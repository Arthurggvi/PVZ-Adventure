Retrieved during this task, 2026-11-21.

Processing: existing assembled frames were resized to exactly 0.5, cropped only in transparent areas and packed into RGBA PNG atlases no larger than 2048 per side. Every original source frame and its order is retained in SpriteFrames; duplicate bitmaps share texture rectangles. No AI-generated artwork or painted replacement pixels were added.
PNG files do not contain playback timing. The 30 ms frame interval follows the source site's downloadable GIF previews where available; animations.json records timing evidence and any fallback per action. This is not a claim that the original game PAM had that precise timebase.
Animation canvas and foot-point alignment: see animations.json. The source does not provide original skeleton/pivots. Death is already headless and missing one arm. Separate armless walking/eating art is not present.
